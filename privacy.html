Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

1. What this demo stores

TaskFlow, as presented in this portfolio build, stores all task, profile and settings data locally in your browser using localStorage. Nothing is transmitted to a server, because this version does not yet include a live backend.

2. Information collected in a production version

A deployed version of TaskFlow would collect account details you provide at sign-up (name, email, password hash), task content you create, and basic usage data to improve reliability.

  • Account information: name, email, profile photo
  • Task data: titles, descriptions, due dates, attachments
  • Usage data: feature interactions, device type, general location

3. How information would be used

Solely to operate the service: authenticating you, syncing tasks across devices, sending reminders you've opted into, and improving product reliability. We would never sell personal data to third parties.

4. Your choices

You can export or delete your data at any time from Settings. Deleting your account removes your tasks and profile information permanently.

5. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to privacy@taskflow.app.
